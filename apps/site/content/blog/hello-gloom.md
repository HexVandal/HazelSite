So. A blog.

Not the LinkedIn kind, not the "thought leadership" kind. Just a place to put
the stuff that rattles around in my skull until it gets too loud. If you're
reading this, either you're lost or you actually care, and both are fine.

## Why bother

Because posting into the void is cheaper than therapy and slightly more legal.
Also because I keep having the same conversations with myself at 2 AM and
eventually it becomes efficient to write the conclusions down.

A few ground rules I'm making up right now:

- I write when I feel like it, not on a schedule.
- Typos stay. They're part of the aesthetic.
- No "in this article we will explore" corporate filler.
- If a post is boring, that's the post's problem.

## What actually lives here

Rambles, mostly. Occasionally something useful — a build note, a thing I broke,
a thing I fixed, a take that aged badly within a week. The tags are there if
you want to skip the noise, though honestly the noise is the point.

> The unexamined life is not worth living, but the over-examined one gets
> posted at 3 AM.

## The stack, briefly

This site is Next.js with Tailwind and a pile of components I keep rewriting
instead of sleeping. Posts are plain markdown files. If I ever add a database
for a blog with this much traffic, please come to my house and take my keyboard
away.

```ts
const groundRules = ["write when it feels right", "ship it anyway", "no filler"];
```

That's it. That's the first post. Let's see how long this lasts.
